I am a postdoctoral scholar in the Department of Physics at Kent State University, working with [Andrew Hanlon](https://www.kent.edu/physics/profile/andrew-hanlon). I received my Ph.D. in Physics from Central China Normal University in 2025, where I worked with [Heng-Tong Ding](https://inspirehep.net/authors/1259382?ui-citation-summary=true). From 2022 to 2025, I was a visitor in the Physics Department at Brookhaven National Laboratory, working with [Swagato Mukherjee](https://inspirehep.net/authors/1036483?ui-citation-summary=true).

I use lattice quantum chromodynamics (QCD) to study hadron structure. My published work includes pion and kaon electromagnetic form factors and pion generalized parton distributions (GPDs). I am now investigating nucleon parton distributions and GPDs, and have begun working on hadron spectroscopy. These calculations help connect the quark and gluon structure of hadrons to measurements at facilities such as Jefferson Lab and the future Electron-Ion Collider.
